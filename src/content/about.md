I was gifted an ESP32 microcontroller development board with the challenge to do something music related with it. One thing led to another and I have now vibe coded software for multiple MCUs including Daisy, Teensy, Pico and Ksoloti. I have found AI tools to be very useful in supporting the development of Eurorack circuits, hardware like cable/headphone holders and 3D printed or PCB based custom front panels. Also worth a mention are the N8Synth solderable breadboards which I have found to be very handy for getting a robust design in the rack for use alongside modules built from commercial kits.

The text and video content on this website currently lags behind the detail available on my GitHub. Please do take a look there for my latest project work.
